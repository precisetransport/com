Welcome to Precise Academy

Transport and Society Online is a richly packaged publication by the Precise Transport and Logistics Academy Nigeria Limited. The publication which has been on for over 5 years cover documents relating to transport and logistics such as - news, research works, events, training, seminars and conferences, documentary, projects, programs, etc,
