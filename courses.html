Comprehensive CIOTA and CILT accredited programs for a successful career in transport and logistics

EDUCATION & TRAINING FOR A PROFESSIONAL CAREER IN TRANSPORT & LOGISTICS

Our academy offers comprehensive professional examination programs accredited by the Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (CILT) and the Chartered Institute of Transport Administration of Nigeria (CIOTA). These programs are designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills needed for a successful career in the transport and logistics industry.

EDUCATION POLICY AND COURSE OBJECTIVES

The Chartered Institute of Transportation Administration of Nigeria (CIOTA) acknowledges the need for improving the standard of effective Transport Management covering all modes of transport (road, rail, maritime, aviation and pipeline). Our education policy is designed to raise the standard of professionalism within Transport management at all levels, encouraging those commencing a career within the industry and improving the knowledge and skills of those already engaged in Transport.

Our syllabus incorporates both theory and practical experience, providing an integrated and progressive system towards Professional Graduate Diploma work with emphasis on professional competence. As a professional transportant, obtaining a credible professional transport qualification offers great opportunities for jobs not only in Nigeria but also within the West Africa (ECOWAS) sub-region and beyond.

THE FOUR MAIN CATEGORIES OF PROFESSIONAL EXAMINATIONS

Our professional certification follows a structured four-level approach designed to progressively build your expertise in transport management and administration.

A. FOUNDATION CERTIFICATE

in Transport Management and Administration

Entry Requirements:

  • Five (5) Credit passes in GCE/SSCE, O'Level or its equivalents in English Language, Mathematics, Economics, and any two Science or Social science subjects
  • Candidates with 5 years of experience can be accepted with 4 Credit Passes including Mathematics and English Language

B. PROFESSIONAL DIPLOMA

in Transport Management and Administration

Entry Requirements:

  • CIOTA/CILT Foundation / Intermediate Certificate of a recognized body
  • Accredited Diploma in Transport Management, Shipping Management, Travel & Tourism, Freight forwarding, Economics, Geography, Marketing, Business Studies, etc.

C. PROFESSIONAL ADVANCE DIPLOMA

in Transport Management and Administration (Associate Membership Grade Level)

Entry Requirements:

  • CIOTA/CILT Professional Diploma, Professional Diploma Part 1 Certificate
  • Advance Diploma, HND, B.Sc., PGD/MBA and other relevant qualifications with at least 4 years working experience

D. POSTGRADUATE PROFESSIONAL DIPLOMA

in Transport Management and Administration (Full Membership Grade Level)

Entry Requirements:

  • CIOTA/CILT Professional Advance Diploma or final Examination Certificate of recognized allied bodies
  • HND, B.Sc., PGD/M.Sc./MBA with at least 2 years working experience in relevant fields

Professional Research Project

To complete the Final Stage Qualifying Examinations, candidates will also be required to complete a professional research project in transport. The project is the academic equivalent of a reputable university postgraduate degree project in Nigeria and Overseas. Successful completion of the Final stage examinations, with research project and internship qualifies you for the award "Full Membership" of the Institute; and gives you eligibility to apply for registration as a Chartered Transportant and also for range of postgraduate programmes at various Universities in Nigeria and Overseas.
